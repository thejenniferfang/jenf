Jennifer Fang

Updated September 2026

I was born in Guangzhou, grew up in Austin, and studied data science at Berkeley. I have lived in the Bay Area, Chicago and New York.

Through August 2026 I was the founding design engineer at Scope, a YC company building observability for AI agents. The work was agent experience: what it is actually like to hand a task to Claude Code or Codex or Cursor, and what it costs you when that goes sideways. I sat with more than twenty engineering teams and kept hearing the same two things. A reasoning trace becomes unreadable at exactly the length where you need to read it, and a tool call that failed looks identical to one that is merely slow. Those conversations turned into what the product collects. I also built the marketing pages, the onboarding, and the internal tools we ran demos on. Half the people who came to a launch event became design partners.

Before that I did supply chain analysis at Tesla and Nissan, and prompt engineering and consulting at IBM, where I still consult. I run Project E, a community of about 1,500 founders, operators and designers, and the thirty-odd dinners and run clubs it has put on across four cities. I was president of the Cal boxing club for two years, which taught me more about running a thing than any of the rest of it did.

Everything below started the same way. A note on my phone about something I liked, kept until the note stopped being readable, and then a site that made it readable again. Two of them turned into tools.

Reach me by email, or find me on X and Instagram.
